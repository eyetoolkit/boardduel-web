How students join

  1. Open the shared link (or visit /games/<board>/?c=CODE).
  2. Two players share one board: the first to open becomes host, the second joins the same room.
  3. No login. Each board is a private room that expires on its own.
FERPA-light. This lab stores no student names and no results. Room codes are created server-side and expire automatically. Upgrade to Teacher Pro (a future build) for live standings and per-board insights — without changing how students join.
