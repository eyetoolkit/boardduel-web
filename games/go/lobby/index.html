Chinese rules

Territory + stones count, no dead-stone removal needed. White gets 7.5 komi, so the game is never a draw by komi.

Byoyomi clock

10 min main time, then five 1-minute byoyomi periods. Spend a period per move; run out and you lose on time.

How to playBlack first → surround → two passes end the game.

1Place a stoneOn any intersection of the grid — one per turn, black goes first.
2Capture by surroundingCut off every liberty of a group and its stones are yours.
3Mind ko & suicideYou can't repeat a ko right after taking one, and you can't fill your own last liberty.
4Pass to scoreWhen neither side can gain, both pass. Territory decides, plus 7.5 komi to white.
