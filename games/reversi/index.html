01 — BOARD LANGUAGE

Six states, one flat plate.

Fourth board, fourth silhouette. Gomoku was a framed battlefield, tictactoe an open hash, connect4 a punched cage. Reversi is the flattest of the family: a plate, a hairline grid, four intersection dots at the center. And its signature event is new — the pieces themselves turn.

05 — WHAT THE SERVER ACTUALLY DOES

Three findings. Two are dead links.

Verified against the live site today — not assumptions. This draft's data is produced by a local engine specifically because of finding #1.

P0 · production

The page and the engine are both missing

/games/reversi/ and /games/reversi/ai.js return HTTP 404 — verified today. The family switcher links to a page that doesn't exist. There is nothing to patch; there is everything to build.

Ship page + engine before the nav. The page pins nothing yet, so the interface contract is still ours to write: newGame · applyMove · chooseMove · SIZE.

P1 · distribution

One live game, four dead links

The homepage links only tictactoe. The gomoku page 404s. Connect4's engine 404s. Reversi is absent entirely. Five games in the family, one shipped.

Ship the finished engines first, then let the switcher follow. A five-game nav over one game is a promise the server can't keep.

P2 · pattern

When it ships, skip the costume

Both live engines fake thinking delays — baseByDiff*2.2 + Math.random()*500 (tictactoe) and *1.8 + Math.random()*600 (connect4). A reversi engine at these depths answers in milliseconds.

No fake wait: the board's own turn is the pacing. And the pieces are radial discs, never emoji — the pattern is documented on two live pages already.

06 — DESIGN NOTES

What reversi adds to the family.

a
Fill carries allegiance, four boards running. Black/white stones, X/O marks, dark/paper checkers, dark/paper discs — the rule has never broken: pieces stay neutral of faction color.
b
Gold is the count, once. No line to draw, so the family's scarcest token goes around the final total. Ring, strike, connector, count — same scarcity, new shape.
c
Fourth silhouette: the flat plate. Framed battlefield → open hash → punched cage → plate with hairline grid and four intersection dots. The furniture changes per game; the tokens never do.
d
Coordinates return. Gomoku reads column-row, tictactoe a 1–9 keypad, connect4 columns only. Reversi restores the full grid — a1–h8 on both axes, the notation players already know.
e
Certainty = captures, not outcomes. The one hint that's exact the instant a position exists. The panel says so — arithmetic is honest in a way prediction can't be.
f
Every number engine-verified — and one honest limit. The showcase replays move-by-move against the engine's own rule set. At equal depth the first player loses 0/20; deeper search wins 20/20. The ladder is the product story, stated in the open.

Open questions

  • Capture badges always on? In casual by default, hidden in ranked rooms, or gated behind "after your first loss"? The count is free to compute.
  • Pass UX: auto-pass with a toast ("no legal move — turn passes"), or an explicit button the player taps?
  • Endgame solver: at ≤10 empties the exact outcome is cheap to compute — reveal "you win by 6 with perfect play" as a countdown? The capture map would graduate into a verdict map.
