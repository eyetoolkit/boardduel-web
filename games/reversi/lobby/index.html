How to playThree in a row — horizontally, vertically or diagonally. That's the whole game.

1Flank to flipPlace a stone so it traps a line of opponent stones between your stones — they all flip to your colour.
2Corners and edgesCorner squares can never be flipped once taken. Edge stones stay put too. Centre is a fight zone.
3Mind the parityWhoever moves last in an empty region keeps those squares. Don't let your opponent fill the board.
4Climb the ladderEvery win moves your Elo across all three Duel sites. Weekly reset keeps it honest.
