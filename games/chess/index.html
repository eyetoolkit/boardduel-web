01 — BOARD LANGUAGE

Six shapes, and the grid disappears.

Fifth board, fifth silhouette. Gomoku was a framed battlefield, tictactoe an open hash, connect4 a punched cage, reversi a flat plate. Chess is the true checker — light and dark squares alternate, and the alternation does the work the grid lines used to. It's also the first family game where the pieces themselves are the alphabet.

The six shapes

Staunton reduced to geometry: crown, coronet, crenellation, mitre, muzzle, head-and-taper. Every other family game needed one shape; chess needs six, and each has to be readable at 40px on either square color.

Allegiance flips — on purpose

Four boards ran dark is you. Chess breaks the rule because chess's own identity outranks the family habit: White moves first, so you're White and paper is you. The exception is the point — pieces never wear faction color either way.

Coordinates return in their native dialect. Gomoku read column-row, tictactoe a keypad, reversi restored a1–h8 — chess goes full SAN: the recorder is a scoresheet, and players already read it fluently.

Last move, tinted

The two squares of the latest move take the soft tint — the from-square empty, the to-square occupied. Chess marks squares, not pieces: the tint survives captures, and nothing about the piece itself needs to change.

Threats, not verdicts

Ember dot: your piece is attacked. Dashed ring and badge: a capture your move can make right now. Attacked ≠ lost — the map lists what's in danger; whether it's truly lost depends on defenders the panel doesn't judge.

The gold line — once

Gomoku rings the five, tictactoe strikes the three, connect4 links the four, reversi counts the total. Chess draws a line: mating piece straight to the king with no way out. Spent once, at mate — nowhere before it.

05 — WHAT THE SERVER ACTUALLY DOES

Three findings. The game is entirely missing.

Verified against the live site today — not assumptions. Chess is the second game whose page and engine are both absent, so this draft's data is a local build end to end.

P0 · production

Page and engine, both 404

/games/chess/ and /games/chess/ai.js return HTTP 404 — verified today. Same shape as reversi: nothing to patch, everything to build. The family switcher links to a page that doesn't exist.

Ship page + engine before the nav. The interface contract is still ours to write: newGame · applyMove · chooseMove · undo, with SAN as the wire format.

P1 · distribution

One live game, four dead links

The homepage links only tictactoe. Gomoku's page 404s, connect4's engine 404s, reversi and chess are absent entirely. Five games in the family, one shipped.

Ship the finished engines first, then let the switcher follow. A five-game nav over one game is a promise the server can't keep.

P2 · honesty about our own build

The demo engine never castled

Material-plus-center evaluation doesn't pay for king safety, so the showcase game contains no castling and no promotion — the search won't spend a tempo it can't score. Real chess players will notice in one game.

A shipped engine adds a king-safety term (castling bonus, pawn shield), or forces castle-search when legal. The rules engine already handles it — the eval is what's honest about being shallow.

06 — DESIGN NOTES

What chess adds to the family.

a
The piece is the alphabet. One stone, one mark, one disc — every earlier game spoke in a single shape. Chess needs six, each carrying its own name, value, and silhouette. The set is drawn once, geometric, readable on both square colors.
b
The allegiance rule bends — deliberately. Dark-is-you held for four boards. Chess's first-move convention is the game's identity, so you're White and paper is you. Family rules serve the game, never the reverse.
c
Fifth silhouette: the true checker. Framed battlefield → open hash → punched cage → flat plate → alternating squares. The grid lines retire; alternation does their work. The furniture changes per game; the tokens never do.
d
SAN closes the coordinates arc. Column-row → keypad → columns → a1–h8 pairs → full algebraic with castling, captures, promotion and #. The recorder is a scoresheet players can read aloud.
e
Certainty steps down to threats. Exact verdicts, horizon verdicts, exact counts — and now attack lists, because chess's tree dwarfs a browser. The panel says exactly what it knows: attacked ≠ lost is written into the copy, not just the data.
f
Every number replay-verified — and one honest limit. All 47 plies replay through the rules engine at build time; the final board is byte-compared before a single pixel renders. deeper search wins 10/10 — every time. at equal depth the first mover wins only 2/10 — color alone decides nothing — depth, not color, decides. And the engine's games never castle: the P2 card owns that limit in public.

Open questions

  • Verdict map at the endgame? At ≤5 pieces, exact mates are cheap to compute in-browser — the capture map could graduate into "you win in 9 with perfect play." The family's certainty ladder, completed.
  • Mate-in-N hints: the search already scores mates — surface "mate in 2" as a hint mode, or keep the game quiet and let the scoresheet tell it after?
  • Promotion UX: auto-queen with an undo toast, or the classic picker? Casual speed vs. the rare underpromotion the picker makes possible.
