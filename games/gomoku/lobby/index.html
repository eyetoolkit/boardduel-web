How to win

  • Place stones on intersections, not in the squares.
  • Columns run A–O, rows 1–15 from the bottom.
  • Black moves first.
  • First to five in a row — horizontally, vertically or diagonally — wins.
  • On touch screens: tap once for the ghost, tap again to commit.
