How to win

  • Place stones on intersections, not in the squares.
  • Columns run A–O, rows 1–15 from the bottom.
  • Black moves first.
  • First to five in a row — across, down or diagonal — wins.
  • On a touch screen: tap once for the ghost, tap again to commit.

How to playOpen → trade → connect five.

1Take the board15×15 grid, 225 intersections. Fresh board every game, coordinates A–O and 1–15.
2Trade stonesBlack opens. A stone never moves once it is placed — every click is a commitment.
3Connect fiveHorizontal, vertical or diagonal. Five is five, whichever way the line runs.
4Read the engineHover the board to see what the engine is weighing. Hints light its strongest answer.
