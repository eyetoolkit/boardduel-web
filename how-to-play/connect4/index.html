How to Play Connect Four

Connect Four is a classic vertical strategy game where two players race to align four discs. Simple rules, surprising depth — easy to learn, hard to master.

The Goal: Drop colored discs into a 7-column, 6-row grid and be the first to connect four of your discs in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

📖 How to Play

Step 1: Set Up the Board

Connect Four is played on a standing board with 7 vertical columns and 6 horizontal rows. The board has openings at the top where discs can be dropped into each column.

Step 2: Take Turns

Red (or Black) always goes first. Players take turns dropping one disc per turn into any column that is not yet full (6 discs maximum per column).

Step 3: Discs Fall to the Bottom

Dropped discs fall to the lowest available empty space in that column — they stack on top of each other. You cannot place a disc mid-air or remove a placed disc.

Step 4: Win by Connecting Four

The first player to get four of their discs in a continuous line — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins. If the board fills without a winner, the game is a draw.

🧠 Winning Strategies

1. Control the Center Column

The center column (column 4) gives you the most possible winning lines. Dominating the center limits your opponent's options and opens the most attack angles.

2. Create Multiple Threats

Position your discs so that you have two ways to win on your next move. Your opponent can only block one — you win automatically with the other.

3. Beware the "7 Above" Trap

Never play directly below a column that has 7 discs stacked. This "gives" your opponent a free winning move on top. Always check what your opponent can do after your move.

4. Think Vertically First

Since discs stack vertically, it's easy to overlook vertical threats. Before dropping a disc, always scan for any columns where your opponent is one disc away from four in a vertical stack.

💡 Pro tip: Connect Four is a solved game — the first player can force a win with perfect play. But in casual play, mistakes are common. Focus on creating double threats (two ways to win at once) and always defend against your opponent's winning moves.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can you win by connecting four in any direction?

Yes. Four in a row can be horizontal (across), vertical (up and down), or diagonal (at an angle). All three directions count as a win.

What happens if the board fills without a winner?

The game is a draw. This is relatively rare in well-played games — most Connect Four games end with a winner well before the board is full.

Is Connect Four a solved game?

Yes, Connect Four has been mathematically solved. With perfect play, the first player (Red) has a forced win. However, finding that perfect strategy is very difficult, so casual games are still highly enjoyable and competitive.
